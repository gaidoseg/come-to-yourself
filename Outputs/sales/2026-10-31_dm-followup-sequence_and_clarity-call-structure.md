## Asset 1: DM Follow-Up Sequence
**Scenario:** Lead DM'd "READY" on Instagram, had a warm initial exchange with Emilie, then went quiet before booking a Free Clarity Call.

**How to use this:** Send from Emilie's personal voice, one message at a time, only to leads who've gone quiet (roughly 3+ days of no reply after the warm exchange). Stop the sequence the moment the lead replies and responds in the moment instead — these are a safety net, not a script to push through regardless of what she says. Skip any message that's already been answered by something she said earlier in the thread.

Timing below is conceptual spacing, not a trigger to automate — Emilie sends these by hand, by feel.

---

### Message 1 — Day 3-4 (gentle check-in, zero pressure)
> Hey [name] 💛 just thinking of you after our chat — no pressure at all, just wanted to check in. How are you feeling about everything we talked about?

**Purpose:** Re-open contact warmly, no ask yet. Lets her reply about life in general if that's where she's at.

---

### Message 2 — Day 6-7 (reintroduce the Clarity Call as the next step)
> No rush on this at all, but if it's still on your mind — I always offer a free Clarity Call first, just to talk through what's going on for you and see if this is actually the right fit before anything else. No pitch, no pressure, just a real conversation. Want me to send you a couple of times that could work?

**Purpose:** Reminds her concretely what the next step is and lowers the stakes of taking it (it's a conversation, not a sales call or a commitment).

---

### Message 3 — Day 9-10 (name the real objection gently, without assuming)
> I know sometimes it's not about not wanting it — it's more "I've tried stuff before and nothing really stuck, so what's actually different this time?" If that's anywhere in your head, totally fair. That's honestly the exact thing I'd want to talk through on a call — not to convince you of anything, just so you're not guessing. 💛

**Purpose:** Surfaces the ICP's most common real objection ("I've already tried therapy/coaching and nothing worked" — `docs/ICP.md`) without putting words in her mouth. Opens the door for Emilie to share her own story (tried therapy, running, yoga, medication before this created lasting change) if the lead engages — per the response strategy already logged in `docs/SALES_PROCESS.md`.

---

### Message 4 — Day 12-13 (low-effort, easy-yes re-ask)
> Still happy to grab 20 minutes whenever works for you — doesn't have to be this week or even next. Just say the word and I'll send over some times. No pressure either way, I just didn't want you to think the door closed. 🙂

**Purpose:** Removes time pressure explicitly (no manufactured urgency), reaffirms the offer is still open, makes saying yes as easy as possible.

---

### Message 5 — Day 14+ (warm, no-pressure close of the sequence)
> I'll leave this here for now so I'm not filling up your DMs 💛 If you ever want to pick this back up — this week, next month, whenever it feels right — I'm here and the offer stands. Sending you love either way.

**Purpose:** Ends the active sequence without guilt-tripping or scarcity language, leaves the relationship warm so she can come back on her own timeline (consistent with no fake urgency/scarcity rule in `docs/BRAND.md`).

## Asset 2: Free Clarity Call Structure
**Purpose:** Simple call outline for Emilie to run the Free Clarity Call — the call itself is the pitch (per `docs/SALES_PROCESS.md`, there's no separate written proposal step today).

### 1. Opening & Rapport (~3-5 min)
- Thank her for making time, acknowledge it can feel vulnerable to get on a call about this stuff.
- Light, human check-in before diving in (how's your day, where are you calling from, etc.) — let her settle before asking anything real.
- Set expectations simply: "This is just a conversation — I want to understand what's going on for you, share a bit about how I work, and by the end we'll both know if this feels right. No pressure either way."

### 2. Discovery (~10-15 min)
Ask open questions, listen more than talk. Grounded in the real pain points from `docs/ICP.md` — let her language lead, don't put words in her mouth first.

- "What's been going on for you lately that made you reach out?"
- "When you say you [overthink everything / feel anxious even when things look fine / lose yourself in others] — what does that actually look like day to day?"
- "How long has this been going on? Has it been getting better, worse, or just... stuck?"
- "What have you already tried to deal with this?" (opens the door naturally to the "tried therapy/coaching before" objection if it's there — let her bring it up, don't assume it)
- "If this didn't change — if you were still feeling this way a year from now — what would that cost you?"
- "What would it actually feel like to trust yourself again / feel calm in your body / have a life that feels like yours?"

Listen for: whether she's genuinely ready for deep, sustained work (vs. looking for a quick fix — per the "who they are NOT" section of `docs/ICP.md`), and reflect back what you're hearing before moving on, so she feels heard, not processed.

### 3. Transition into the Program (~5-7 min)
Bridge naturally from what she just shared — don't pitch cold.

- "From what you're describing, this is exactly the kind of pattern the work I do is built for." (tie it back to 1-2 specific things she said)
- Briefly share your own story if it fits naturally — tried therapy, running, yoga, medication, and what actually created the shift for you (per `docs/BRAND.md` — this is a core trust-building asset, use it).
- Describe "Come Home to Yourself" using only confirmed details from `docs/OFFERS.md`:
  - 1:1, online, 6 months
  - Bi-weekly 1:1 sessions with you
  - 24/7 WhatsApp support between sessions
  - 12 modules delivered on Kajabi, built around the 12-step subconscious transformation process — rewiring subconscious patterns, releasing stored emotional blocks, building self-trust and inner safety, reconnecting with her authentic self
  - Outcome: moving from stuck/overwhelmed/disconnected to grounded, clear, confident — deep self-trust, inner peace, emotional freedom
- Do not state a price in this conversation from this document — pricing isn't finalized in `docs/OFFERS.md` yet. Discuss investment/payment live, directly, in Emilie's own words, once that's defined.
- Do not reference the method's originator or other certified coaches — keep it about Emilie's own story, voice, and client results (per the `docs/BRAND.md` positioning decision, 2026-10-31).

### 4. Checking Fit (~3-5 min)
- Ask directly: "Based on everything we just talked about, how does this feel to you?"
- Let her ask questions — about the process, time commitment, what sessions look like, etc.
- If helpful, reference a testimonial in your own words (e.g. a client who felt calmer and more in control, or who hit every goal she set) — see `My profile/testimonials.md`. Don't fabricate outcomes beyond what's documented there.

### 5. Close (~3-5 min)
There's no formal written proposal step today (per `docs/SALES_PROCESS.md`) — the call itself is the close. Keep it simple and pressure-free:
- If she's a clear yes: talk through next steps live — payment and enrollment details, when she'd start, how WhatsApp/Kajabi access works. Handle this conversationally; there's no fixed script for logistics yet since there's no booking/payment tool in place.
- If she needs time: "Totally understandable — this is a real decision. Take the time you need. I'm here whenever you're ready, no pressure." Agree on a concrete (but soft) next touchpoint, e.g. "Want me to check back in with you in a few days?"
- If it's not the right fit: thank her warmly for her honesty and openness; leave the door open without pushing.
- Either way, end warm: reaffirm that showing up for the call was itself a real step, regardless of outcome.
