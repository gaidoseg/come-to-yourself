## 1. Onboarding Welcome Sequence (Week One)

**Context note:** `docs/SALES_PROCESS.md` confirms there's no CRM/booking tool yet and enrollment happens live on/after the Clarity Call — so this sequence assumes Emilie (or the Client Success Agent on her behalf) sends these manually via WhatsApp/Instagram DM/email, whatever channel the client is already in.

### Step 1 — Welcome message (send same day as enrollment, via WhatsApp)

> Hi [Client Name], I'm so happy to have you here. 🤍
>
> Welcome to Come Home to Yourself — this is the start of six months that I believe will change how you relate to yourself, for good.
>
> I'll send you everything you need to get oriented over the next couple of days, but for now, just take a breath. You made a brave decision saying yes to this, and I'm honored to walk it with you.
>
> This number is also where you can reach me between our sessions — more on that below. Talk soon.
> — Emilie

### Step 2 — What to expect message (send within 1–2 days of welcome message)

> A quick orientation to how our six months together will work:
>
> **Your sessions:** We'll meet 1:1, bi-weekly (every two weeks), for the full length of the program. I'll send the link to book your first session below — once it's set, we'll keep a steady bi-weekly rhythm from there.
>
> **Support between sessions:** You have 24/7 WhatsApp access to me. If something comes up, a pattern surfaces, or you just need to process something between sessions, reach out — this isn't a once-every-two-weeks relationship, it's ongoing.
>
> **Your curriculum:** You'll get access to 12 modules on Kajabi, which guide you through the 12-step process at your own pace alongside our sessions. I'll send your login details separately so you can log in and look around before our first call.
>
> This is a journey, not a quick fix — so there's no need to rush through anything. We'll go at the pace that's right for you.

### Step 3 — First session scheduling (send alongside or immediately after Step 2)

> Since there's no booking system set up yet, scheduling happens by direct message — so: what does your calendar look like over the next week or two? Send me a few days/times that could work and I'll confirm our first session from there.
>
> (Note for Emilie: until a booking tool like Calendly/Cal.com exists — see `docs/SALES_PROCESS.md` — this has to be coordinated manually per client.)

### Step 4 — Kajabi access (send once login is actually provisioned)

> You're all set up — here's your login for the 12 modules: [Kajabi link/credentials].
>
> No pressure to "finish" anything before our first session. Just explore Module 1 if you'd like, and bring whatever comes up.

## 2. Session Prep Template (Generic, Reusable)

**Fill in before each bi-weekly 1:1 session. Copy this block per session until a per-client notes system exists.**

```
SESSION PREP — [Client Name] — Session #[__] — [Date]

1. Since last session
   - What did they say they'd work on / notice between sessions?
   - Anything flagged over WhatsApp since the last call worth following up on?

2. Program position
   - Which Kajabi module(s) are they likely on/around at this point in the 6 months?
   - Any module-specific themes worth checking in on (resistance, breakthroughs, confusion)?

3. Today's session
   - What do I want to ask about progress since last time?
   - What pattern/theme feels most alive for them right now?
   - Is there a specific tool/exercise from the 12-step process that fits where they are?

4. Watch for
   - Signs of stuckness, overwhelm, or disengagement
   - Wins worth naming and reinforcing (these are also testimonial-request moments — see below)

5. After this session
   - One thing to carry into WhatsApp support before the next session
   - Note anything to remember for the next prep sheet
```

**Flag for Emilie:** This is a generic template because there's no per-client data system yet. Once there are active clients, a simple per-client notes file (e.g. one Markdown file per client under something like `Outputs/client-success/clients/[client-name].md`, updated after each session) would be a natural next step — it would let this template actually pull from real history instead of starting blank every time.

## 3. Testimonial Request & Referral Ask Messages

Tone matches the existing testimonials in `My profile/testimonials.md` — short, warm, first-person-anchored, specific rather than generic praise. These are *requests* only; no testimonial should be written or implied on the client's behalf, and nothing gets added to `testimonials.md` until Emilie receives and approves the client's actual words.

### Testimonial request (send after a strong session, or near end of program)

> Hi [Client Name] — I just want to pause and say how proud I am of the work you've done.
>
> If you're open to it, would you be willing to share a few words about your experience in the program? Nothing polished or long — just what's true for you. Something like: what shifted for you, or how you feel now compared to when we started.
>
> It would mean a lot, and it helps other women who are standing where you were six months ago find their way here too. Totally okay if now isn't the right time — just let me know. 🤍

### Referral ask (send to a client who's clearly thriving / has said something positive unprompted)

> Hi [Client Name] — hearing you talk about [specific shift they mentioned] made my day. That's exactly the kind of change I hope for every client.
>
> If you know anyone in your life who's stuck in the same place you were — overthinking, self-doubt, feeling disconnected from themselves — and could use this kind of support, I'd be so grateful if you passed my name along. No pressure at all, just know the door's open for anyone you care about.
