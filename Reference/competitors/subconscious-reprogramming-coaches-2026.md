## Question asked
Who are 5–8 real, currently-active coaches/programs in the subconscious reprogramming / nervous-system regulation / shadow work / anxiety-and-self-trust coaching space for women, marketing primarily on Instagram or TikTok — and how does Emilie's "Come Home to Yourself" compare on positioning, format, length, price, and approach?

## Findings

### 1. Angelica Rose — Subconscious Mind Reprogramming & Manifestation
- **Handle:** [@thesubconsciousboss](https://www.instagram.com/thesubconsciousboss/) (Instagram)
- **Positioning:** Subconscious mind reprogramming + manifestation coaching.
- **Format/length/price:** Not publicly disclosed in available search results.
- **Distinctive vs. Emilie:** Leans into "manifestation" framing (law-of-attraction adjacent) rather than nervous-system/somatic language. No stated structured multi-step method comparable to the 12-step Human Instrument® process — more content-led than program-led as far as public info shows.
- Source: [Instagram search result](https://www.instagram.com/thesubconsciousboss/)

### 2. Caitlin Clark — Somatic Shadow Work Coach
- **Handle:** [@complete_bycaitlin](https://www.instagram.com/complete_bycaitlin/) (~4,400 followers per search snapshot)
- **Positioning:** "Somatic Shadow Work" — healing unconscious patterns for better relationships.
- **Format/length/price:** Not disclosed in available sources.
- **Distinctive vs. Emilie:** Shadow-work framing (Jungian/unconscious-pattern language) rather than "subconscious reprogramming"; relationship-outcome focus rather than Emilie's self-trust/inner-peace framing.
- Source: search result listing, [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/complete_bycaitlin/)

### 3. Ludovica Colella — Confidence Coaching for People-Pleasers
- **Handle:** [@ludovica_colella_coaching](https://lu-cole.com/) / [lu-cole.com](https://lu-cole.com/private-coaching/confidence-coaching-for-people-pleasers)
- **Positioning:** CBT-trained (postgrad diploma in CBT, master's in psychoanalysis) confidence coach helping women "break free from people-pleasing, set boundaries without guilt, reclaim their voice."
- **Format & price (publicly listed):** Session-based packages, not a fixed-length container program — 10-Session Transformation Package at £150/session (~£1,500 total); 20-Session Deep Transform Package (~£3,000 total); single sessions £165. Email support included, not 24/7.
- **Distinctive vs. Emilie:** Clinical/CBT credibility angle (explicitly therapy-adjacent credentials) vs. Emilie's subconscious/nervous-system + certification-based positioning. Pay-per-session model vs. Emilie's fixed 6-month container. Lower price-per-touchpoint, no stated between-session support channel.
- Sources: [lu-cole.com private coaching page](https://lu-cole.com/private-coaching/confidence-coaching-for-people-pleasers), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ludovica-colella-53a633129/)

### 4. Lovisa Engstrand / The Exhale Collective — Nervous System Regulation Coach for Women
- **Handle:** [@lovisaaengstrand](https://www.instagram.com/lovisaaengstrand/) / [theexhalecollective.co](https://www.theexhalecollective.co/)
- **Positioning:** "Nervous System Regulation Coach for Women" — serves "wired but tired" high-functioning women in burnout/survival mode.
- **Format/length:** 1:1 coaching plus a "Calm & Resilient" course and a 14-day reset; client testimonials suggest coaching containers of roughly 4–5 months; also runs a podcast ("The Breathing Room") and paid masterclasses (e.g., ~$20 CAD "Shake & Settle").
- **Price:** Full 1:1 price not publicly listed; only the $20 CAD masterclass price is public.
- **Distinctive vs. Emilie:** Built on an explicit named framework ("Regulate & Restore Framework" — stress, sleep, movement, nutrition, connection) rooted in polyvagal theory/breath science/somatics — a body-first, multi-pillar wellness model vs. Emilie's subconscious-pattern/emotional-release model. Has a lower-ticket content funnel (podcast, $20 masterclass, course) before 1:1 — Emilie's funnel currently jumps straight from free content to a free Clarity Call to the full 6-month program.
- Sources: [theexhalecollective.co](https://www.theexhalecollective.co/)

### 5. Releaseology — Nervous System Coaching Program
- **Handle:** [@releaseology](https://www.instagram.com/releaseology/) (per site) / [releaseology.com](https://www.releaseology.com/challenge-page/nervoussystemcoaching)
- **Positioning:** Science-based nervous system reset for "high-performing women, mothers, caregivers, empaths" dealing with chronic stress, anxiety, burnout, trauma.
- **Format:** 1:1 coaching (Zoom/phone) combining talk-based regulation + "virtual body-based treatments," plus daily text/email check-ins and weekly sessions. Bundled bonuses: a nervous-system course (stated value $694) and 3 months of an app (stated value $49.99).
- **Price:** $3,000 (publicly listed).
- **Distinctive vs. Emilie:** Polyvagal-theory/body-based-treatment framing rather than subconscious reprogramming; shorter/cheaper than Emilie's program if her price lands above $3,000; daily check-ins (similar to Emilie's 24/7 WhatsApp) but via text/email rather than WhatsApp specifically.
- Source: [Releaseology program page](https://www.releaseology.com/challenge-page/nervoussystemcoaching)

### 6. Laura Larios — Nervous System Coach for High-Performing Women
- **Handle:** [@lauralisalarios](https://www.instagram.com/lauralisalarios/) / [lauralisalarios.com](https://lauralisalarios.com/)
- **Positioning:** Works with women "wired beneath the calm" — high performers in corporate leadership/entrepreneurship; credibility built on external collaboration with Stanford Cardiovascular Health and use of biodata (wearables) + the Safe & Sound Protocol.
- **Format/length:** 3-month 1:1 program (per case-study language); also co-runs a separate $5,997 "NeuroSomatics" practitioner-certification program (that one is for coaches, not end clients — don't conflate with her client offer).
- **Price (client program):** Not publicly disclosed.
- **Distinctive vs. Emilie:** Data/biometric-driven credibility angle (wearables, clinical collaboration) — more "neuroscience/clinical" positioning than Emilie's warmer, poetic, spiritual-leaning brand voice. Targets high-performing/corporate women specifically, a narrower professional-identity niche than Emilie's broader "women stuck in anxiety/overthinking."
- Sources: [lauralisalarios.com](https://lauralisalarios.com/), [podcast feature](https://broadwaypodcastnetwork.com/podcasts/was-it-chance/66-laura-larios-it-s-a-called-a-nervous-system-for-a-reason/66)

### 7. Natasha Levinger — Inner Child Healing / "Inner Child Reprogramming"
- **Site:** [natashalevinger.com](https://www.natashalevinger.com/offerings) — published author of *Healing Your Inner Child: Re-Parenting Yourself for a More Secure & Loving Life* (2023, mainstream publisher — a notable credibility/distribution advantage).
- **Positioning:** "Therapeutic intuitive" / energy reader blending inner-child healing with self-love and self-trust language, explicitly uses "reprogramming" terminology (e.g., "Inner Child Reprogramming" meditation offer).
- **Format/price:** Mix of a 4-week group "Inner Child Healing" class (~$333, payment plans available) and various named 1:1 offerings (psychic mentorship, healing mentorship, async voice-note/email support tiers) — individual 1:1 pricing not consistently published.
- **Distinctive vs. Emilie:** Explicitly blends intuitive/energy-reading and psychic framing with reprogramming language — more esoteric/spiritual than Emilie's nervous-system-and-subconscious-pattern framing, and has a published book as a trust asset Emilie doesn't currently have. Offers async support tiers (voice note, email) as lower-cost alternatives to full 1:1 — a funnel structure Emilie's single 6-month 1:1 offer doesn't have.
- Sources: [Offerings page](https://www.natashalevinger.com/offerings), [book listing](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/healing-your-inner-child-natasha-levinger/1142727879)

### 8. Shimrit Nativ / "MYP" — originator of the Human Instrument® Mastery Method (important flag, not a typical competitor)
- **Handle:** [@shimritnativ](https://www.shimritnativ.com/) / [shimritnativ.com](https://www.shimritnativ.com/coaching-certification-program)
- **What this is:** Shimrit Nativ created the **Human Instrument® Mastery (HIM) Method** — the same 12-step, mind-body-spirit framework Emilie is certified in. Nativ runs her own coaching offers (e.g., a "Holistic Breakthrough" 1:1 session at €4,444, and a 9-month "RISE Mastermind" at €2,800) and also certifies other coaches ("MYP Coaching Certification") who then deliver the identical HIM 12-step program to their own clients.
- **Why this matters:** Anyone who DMs "CONNECT" to @shimritnativ's Instagram is matched for free with a certified MYP/HIM coach — meaning Emilie is not the only certified deliverer of this exact method, and there is likely a cohort of other HIM-certified coaches marketing the same 12-step framework on Instagram. This is a **direct methodology overlap**, not just a thematic one.
- **Judgment call flagged for Emilie:** Is Nativ's own brand (and the wider pool of other HIM-certified coaches) a competitive risk to differentiate against, or a credibility asset to lean into (e.g., naming the certification lineage explicitly as third-party proof of a proven method)? This needs Emilie's call — Research Agent is not deciding it.
- Sources: [MYP Coaching Certification Program](https://www.shimritnativ.com/coaching-certification-program), [Current Offers](https://www.shimritnativ.com/current-offers)

## What's still unknown
- **No verified follower counts, engagement rates, or posting cadence** for any competitor — Instagram login/API access wasn't available to this research pass; counts cited (e.g., Caitlin Clark's ~4,400) are from indirect search snapshots and may be stale.
- **Full 1:1 pricing is missing for most "nervous system" competitors** (Exhale Collective, Laura Larios, Angelica Rose, Caitlin Clark) — only partial/bundled or practitioner-certification prices were publicly findable; their actual end-client containers may price above or below Emilie's eventual number.
- **No confirmed competitor who matches Emilie's exact combination** (6-month 1:1 + bi-weekly sessions + 24/7 WhatsApp + 12-step branded subconscious method + Kajabi curriculum) was found — closest analogues (Releaseology, Exhale Collective) differ in session cadence, support channel, or framework language.
- **Whether other Human Instrument®/MYP-certified coaches are actively marketing on Instagram and how many** — this needs a dedicated follow-up search specifically for "certified HIM coach" or "certified MYP coach" + Instagram.
- **TikTok-specific competitors** weren't separately surveyed in this pass (search results skewed Instagram/website); a TikTok-focused follow-up could surface a different competitor set given how much "nervous system regulation" content lives there (see the content-trends file).
