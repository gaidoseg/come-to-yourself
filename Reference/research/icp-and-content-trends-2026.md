## Part A — Where women look for help, and what language they use

### Question asked
Where do women searching for help with "overthinking," "anxiety," "people-pleasing," "self-trust," and "nervous system regulation" actually look online, and what phrases/language do they use to describe the problem?

### Findings
- **"Nervous system regulation" has become a mainstream, non-clinical search/social term**, not just a therapy-office concept. Multiple 2026 wellness-trend roundups independently name it as a top trend: "neurowellness" was named the #1 2026 wellness trend at the Global Wellness Summit, and nervous-system regulation is described as moving from therapy offices into mainstream women's health spaces. Sources: [Global Wellness Summit trend piece](https://www.globalwellnesssummit.com/trendium/neurowellness-one-of-the-biggest-wellness-trends-of-2026/), [NewBeauty](https://www.newbeauty.com/view/nervous-system-regulation), [2Life Health](https://www.2lifehealth.com/post/the-great-nervous-system-reset-2026-s-powerful-return-to-natural-wellness).
- **One source (FlowlyOS blog) claims Google searches for "nervous system regulation" are up "nearly 1,000% year-over-year"** — flagged as a claim to treat cautiously: the article states this figure but cites no underlying data source or methodology. Do not repeat this stat externally as a verified fact without finding the primary source. Source: [FlowlyOS blog](https://blog.flowlyos.com/nervous-system-regulation-complete-guide/) (unsourced claim within the article).
- **TikTok has a large, active "nervous system" content ecosystem**: the hashtag #nervoussystemhealing alone had roughly 178,500 tagged videos per a Fast Company report on the trend, with the format often being creators demonstrating somatic self-regulation techniques on camera ("shaking out," vagus-nerve exercises) in real time. Source: [Fast Company, "Everyone on TikTok is 'regulating their nervous system'"](https://www.fastcompany.com/91485280/everyone-on-tiktok-is-regulating-their-nervous-system).
- **The underlying psychological framing women search for is consistent across sources**: people-pleasing is repeatedly described (by therapists/psychologists writing publicly) as a fear-driven, learned safety response ("fawn" response) rather than a personality trait — language like "fear of disappointing someone," "feeling unsafe," "low self-esteem," "needing approval," and "losing yourself" recurs. Overthinking is consistently framed as a nervous-system/anxiety symptom — "a defense mechanism to cope with anxiety," keeping the sympathetic (fight-or-flight) system chronically activated. Sources: [AK Psychotherapy, "Rebuilding Self-Trust After Years of People Pleasing & Self-Abandonment"](https://www.ak-psychotherapy.com/blog-posts/self-trust), [Psychology Today, "People-pleasing is a flawed way to exert control"](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/perfectionism/202510/people-pleasing-is-a-flawed-way-to-exert-control).
- **Self-trust content consistently frames the "fix" in body-based, nervous-system terms, not just cognitive ones** — e.g., "scheduling time alone teaches your nervous system you're safe with yourself," "practicing boundaries teaches your nervous system it's safe to protect your energy." This maps closely onto Emilie's own positioning (nervous-system/subconscious work, not just mindset talk). Source: therapist/coach blog content aggregated in search (see citations above).
- **Where they look, concretely:** the strongest, most directly observable evidence is TikTok (hashtag volume) and Instagram (competitor density — see Part B and the competitor file) as the primary public-search-and-discovery surfaces; Substack newsletters from therapists/coaches (e.g., Natalie Lue, Mariana Orkenyi, AK Psychotherapy–adjacent writers) also surfaced repeatedly as a place this audience reads reflective, longer-form content on these exact topics — suggesting Substack/newsletter content is a secondary but real channel for this audience beyond Instagram/TikTok. Source: search result pattern across multiple Substack domains (see queries above).

### What's still unknown

This means direct, verbatim audience language from r/anxiety, r/CPTSD, r/selfimprovement, etc. is **not yet verified** — only secondary/therapist-filtered language is cited above. A follow-up pass with direct Reddit access (or a tool that can read Reddit) is recommended before treating any specific "forum quote" as real.

## Part B — What's working on Instagram for coaches in this niche (2025–2026)

### Question asked
What content formats and hooks are currently working on Instagram in 2025–2026 for coaches in the emotional-healing/mindset/nervous-system niche?

### Findings
- **"Recognition hooks" are outperforming transformation-promise hooks.** The pattern that converts now is describing the viewer's internal pattern precisely enough that they feel identified, not sold to — e.g., naming a specific, oddly-specific failure state ("if a delayed text reorganizes your entire afternoon, that's not overreacting, that's a nervous system doing its job badly") rather than a generic promise ("heal your nervous system"). Source: [Blitzcut, "Trending Instagram Reel Hooks for Fitness and Mindset Creators (2026)"](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/fitness-mindset-reel-hooks-2026).
- **A dedicated hook library exists specifically for coaches/therapists/female entrepreneurs** as its own content category in 2026 — suggesting this sub-niche (coaches speaking to women) has recognizable, repeatable hook patterns distinct from general mindset content. Source: [Blitzcut, "Reels Hooks for Coaches, Therapists and Female Entrepreneurs (2026)"](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/reel-hooks-coaches-therapists-2026).
- **Abstract mindset statements no longer perform** — generic lines like "your mind is your greatest asset" are described as indistinguishable from thousands of competing Reels and are named explicitly as "no longer working." This is directly relevant to Emilie's brand voice guidance to avoid generic "manifest your dream life" language — the trend data backs up that instinct. Source: [Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/fitness-mindset-reel-hooks-2026).
- **Raw, low-production "YAP video" style content is outperforming polished posts** — off-the-cuff, talking-head selfie videos with subtitles/text headline, minimal editing. This fits Emilie's "warm, grounded" (not hypey) brand voice better than a highly produced aesthetic would. Source: [Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/fitness-mindset-reel-hooks-2026); corroborated by general 2026 trend roundups noting authenticity-over-polish. Source: [Later, "Top Instagram Reels Trends 2026"](https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-trends/).
- **Hook speed matters more than before** — the hold-rate signal is now reported to fire within the first 1.5 seconds, meaning the opening line/text must land immediately or the viewer scrolls past before the 3-second mark. Source: [Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/fitness-mindset-reel-hooks-2026).
- **Format variety beyond straight talking-head**: direct-to-camera storytimes, fast-cut edits, and "game-style" formats (this-or-that, blind rankings) are named as notable 2026 Reels trends generally (not niche-specific, but applicable). Source: [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-trends/), [Manychat, "2026 Instagram Trends"](https://manychat.com/blog/instagram-trends-for-creators-2026/).
- **TikTok's nervous-system content format is demonstrative, not just verbal** — the viral format is creators physically showing a somatic regulation technique in the moment (shaking out tension, vagus-nerve exercises), which is a format Instagram Reels in this niche could borrow but that's distinct from a pure talking-head hook. Source: [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91485280/everyone-on-tiktok-is-regulating-their-nervous-system).

### What's still unknown
- No data here is Emilie-account-specific (her own Reels/engagement weren't analyzed in this pass — that would require Instagram account access, not just public web search).
- Posting cadence recommendations and hashtag/SEO specifics for this niche weren't deeply verified beyond general 2026 trend articles — a follow-up pass reading the full Later/Manychat/Newengen trend pages in detail (rather than search snippets) would sharpen this.
- No comparison of organic Reels performance vs. carousel/static posts specifically for this niche — most sources found were Reels-focused.
