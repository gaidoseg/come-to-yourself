## Who they are
- **Gender:** Women
- **Where they spend time online:** Instagram (primary — program is promoted via @emiliegaidos and Instagram DMs)
- **Demographics:** _age range, life stage, profession not yet specified — Research Agent to investigate_

## What they struggle with
- Overthinking everything — mentally exhausted from it
- Struggle to trust themselves, constantly seek validation from others
- Feel anxious or disconnected even when life looks "fine" from the outside
- People-pleasing — put others first and lose themselves in the process
- Have tried things before (therapy, self-help, etc.) without lasting change

## What they want
- To feel calm in their body
- To trust themselves deeply
- To live a life that "actually feels like theirs"
- Emotional freedom and inner peace
- Deep, lasting change — not another temporary fix

## Objections they likely raise before buying
_(Not yet documented from real conversations — Sales Agent should log real objections here as they come up.)_

| Objection | How we respond |
|---|---|
| "I've already tried therapy/coaching and nothing worked" | Lean on Emilie's own story — she tried therapy, running, yoga, medication before this method created lasting change |
| "It's too expensive" | Program is €2,400 (or €450/mo x 6) — priced low-mid for the market intentionally while Emilie is early-stage; a payment plan exists. Frame as cost of 6 months of staying stuck vs. investing once, not as a hard sell. |
