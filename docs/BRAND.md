# Brand Voice & Positioning

> Read by: Content, Marketing, Sales agents — keeps every piece of writing sounding like the same business.
> Source: `My profile/About Emilie Gaidos.pdf` and `Reference/brand-assets/Come Home to Yourself - landing page.html`.

## Positioning
- **Business name:** Emilie Gaidos Coaching (Sweden)
- **Program name:** "Come Home to Yourself"
- **Tagline / one-liner:** "For women ready to come home to themselves."
- **Unique value proposition:** A certified **Human Instrument® Mastery Coach** guiding women through a structured 12-step subconscious-reprogramming process — not surface-level talk therapy or generic mindset coaching, but deep nervous-system and emotional-pattern work for lasting change.
- **Niche:** Subconscious reprogramming / nervous-system & emotional-pattern coaching for women stuck in anxiety, overthinking, people-pleasing, and self-doubt.

## Voice & Tone
- **3 words that describe the voice:** Warm, grounded, poetic (editorial, not clinical or hypey)
- **Do:** Speak in first person, validate the reader's inner experience before offering the shift ("you're not alone... this doesn't have to be your normal"), use short emotionally-resonant sentences, contrast "where it starts" vs "where you're headed," lead with story/felt experience over bullet-point claims.
- **Don't:** No corporate/clinical jargon, no hard-sell urgency tactics, no generic "manifest your dream life" language — keep it specific to nervous system, subconscious patterns, and self-trust.
- **Recurring phrases/motifs:** "come home to yourself," "lead from love, not fear," "deep self-trust," "inner peace," "emotional freedom," "safe being yourself."

## Visual Identity
- **Colors:** Brown-deep `#3A2A1E`, Brown `#6B4A34`, Brown-soft `#9C7A5C`, Beige `#F4ECDD`, Beige-deep `#E9DCC3`, White `#FFFFFF`
- **Fonts:** Headings — Lora (serif); Body/UI — Jost (sans-serif, light weight)
- **Visual style:** Editorial single-column scroll, alternating beige/white bands, lots of white space, warm/earthy palette, calm and feminine (not bright/loud)
- **Logo / photo assets:** `Reference/brand-assets/`
- **Instagram handle:** @emiliegaidos

## Values (from "My Vision")
- Inner peace **over** perfection
- Self-trust **over** seeking validation
- Authenticity **over** appearance

## Credibility
- **Certification:** Certified Human Instrument® Mastery Coach
- **Method:** 12-step transformation process (subconscious pattern rewiring, releasing stored emotional blocks, building self-trust/inner safety, reconnecting with authentic self)
- **Personal story:** Emilie's own journey through anxiety, depression, panic, and OCD — tried therapy, running, yoga, medication without lasting change, until subconscious/energy work created the shift. This lived-experience story is a core trust-building asset — use it in content, not just the "About" page.
- **Testimonials:** See `My profile/testimonials.md`

> Note: the PDF "About" page mentions Emilie's personal history with "depression, panic, and OCD"; the public landing page softens this to "depression, panic, and anxiety." Default to the landing-page wording in public content unless Emilie says otherwise.

## Positioning decision: Human Instrument® / Shimrit Nativ lineage (2026-10-31)
Research found that Shimrit Nativ created the Human Instrument® Mastery Method and certifies other coaches in it — Emilie is not the only certified deliverer of this exact 12-step framework. **Decision: downplay this.** Keep the "certified Human Instrument® Mastery Coach" credential as-is, but do not point to the method's originator or other certified coaches in content/sales copy. Position Emilie's brand around her own story, voice, and client results rather than the certification lineage.
