## My Story
My path into this work began long before I called it "healing."

For most of my life, I felt anxious and disconnected — constantly trying to prove my worth. I cared deeply for others, but lost myself in the process. Behind a calm surface, I struggled with depression, panic, and anxiety that left me feeling trapped in my own mind. I tried everything — therapy, running, yoga, medication — but nothing created lasting change.

Still, there was always a quiet inner voice saying: *"I just know there has to be more to life than this."* That voice became my turning point.

I began exploring the subconscious mind, energy work, and deeper self-connection. For the first time, I felt safe within myself. I stopped fighting my emotions and started meeting them with love instead of fear.

I realized that healing isn't about fixing ourselves — it's about remembering who we are beneath the patterns, the fear, and the conditioning. Today, I guide women through that same transformation.

## My Method
As a certified Human Instrument® Mastery Coach, I guide clients through a 12-step transformation process designed to create deep, lasting change — not surface-level coaching. Inside the work:
- Rewiring subconscious patterns that drive anxiety and self-doubt
- Releasing emotional blocks stored in the body
- Building deep self-trust and inner safety
- Reconnecting with the authentic self

## My Vision
Every woman deserves to feel safe in her own body, confident in her truth, and free to create a life that feels aligned — not forced. I stand for inner peace over perfection, self-trust over seeking validation, and authenticity over appearance.
